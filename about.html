Our Story

Founded in 2018, The Rustic Spoon began with a simple philosophy: honor the ingredient, respect the grower, and serve with passion.

Executive Chef Marcus Vance partners exclusively with regional farmers and organic purveyors to ensure that every dish highlights the authentic flavors of the season. Our kitchen blends classic culinary techniques with modern innovation to create an atmosphere of warmth and sophistication.

We invite you to sit back, relax, and share a wonderful meal with the people you love.
